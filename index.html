New homes, additions and small commercial buildings. We start from your brief, your site and your budget, and work toward a design you can price and build.

  • Concept options
  • Plans, elevations, sections

Interior design

Layouts, finishes, lighting and millwork for whole homes or single rooms, resolved in 3D so you approve what you can see.

  • Space planning
  • Material and finish palette
  • Kitchen and millwork drawings
  • Room-by-room renders

Permit drawings

Clear, coordinated drawing sets for building permit applications, with revisions handled when plan review comes back.

  • Existing and proposed plans
  • Elevations and sections
  • Details and schedules
  • Responses to review comments

Photoreal stills and short films of exteriors and interiors, for approvals, pre-sales, listings and your own peace of mind.

  • Street and context views
  • Images for marketing

Intern Architect Arif

Architect in BangladeshIntern Architect in Ontario

Arif treats every project as the meeting point of climate, context and culture. He trained at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology and spent his early career designing offices, hospitality, coastal infrastructure and tourism masterplans across Bangladesh.

In Ontario he works on homes: designing them, modelling them in Revit, rendering them to photographic quality and preparing the drawings that take them to permit. He also builds software that makes that work faster.
